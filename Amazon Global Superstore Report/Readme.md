# Amazon-Global-Superstore-Report

# 📊 Amazon Global Superstore Sales Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end e-commerce sales analytics project built using Excel and Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer profitability, product trends, and regional business insights.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw e-commerce sales data into actionable business insights through dashboarding and KPI analysis.

The dashboard helps track overall business performance, identify profitable customer segments, analyze product-level profit and loss, and monitor sales growth trends across regions.

---

## 🔄 Workflow

Excel → Power BI

- Excel: Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Power BI: Data modeling, dashboard creation, and business insights  

---

## 📄 Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)

- Cleaned and prepared 56K+ e-commerce sales records  
- Handled missing values and formatting inconsistencies  
- Organized transactional data for analysis  
- Prepared data for dashboard reporting and KPI tracking  

---

## 📊 Power BI (Dashboard & Insights)

- Built an interactive dashboard with:
  - KPI cards (Total Sales, Quantity, KPI Ratings, Returns)  
  - Monthly sales trend analysis  
  - Sales by segment analysis  
  - Product-level profit and loss analysis  
  - Customer profitability tracking  
  - Region-wise sales distribution map  

- Added interactive filters for:
  - Year  
  - Region  
  - Product categories  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Consumer segment contributed the highest share of total sales  
- Certain products consistently generated losses despite strong sales volume  
- Sales showed steady growth between 2012–2015 with seasonal fluctuations  
- A small group of customers contributed significantly to total profit  
- Regional analysis highlighted strong sales concentration in specific global markets
